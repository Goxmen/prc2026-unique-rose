First, handle Rome’s unusual time records. Then add weather, traffic and stand clues. Finally, keep predictions inside a flight-plan window.

The idea behind the family

Rome has two kinds of recorded taxi time.

Some Rome records use the scheduled time as the time the plane left its stand. Their target includes the delay. A classifier estimates the chance p of this. A regressor estimates taxi seconds.
